How it works
  • Draft — select the weeks to include, then launch Phase 1 when ready.
  • Phase 1 — each recipient is emailed a survey to nominate up to 2 wins per category, per team.
  • Phase 1 Closed — no more Phase 1 nominations are accepted. Review nominations before launching Phase 2.
  • Phase 2 — one consolidated survey per recipient covering all nominated wins; final selection of up to 2 per category.
  • Phase 2 Closed — no more Phase 2 votes are accepted.
  • Wins Declared — the top 2 wins per category are ranked from Phase 2 votes. Download or send the results at any time.
