What is Resource Planner?

Resource Planner is a platform for managing projects, teams, and workforce capacity across your organisation. It brings together project tracking, sprint planning, resource allocation, budgeting, and reporting in one place.

Features

Project Tracking

Manage projects end-to-end with statuses, sub-statuses, types, priorities, and full governance workflows.

Resource Planning

Plan and allocate team members to projects and sprints, track capacity, and prevent over-commitment.

Sprint & Capacity Planning

Define sprints within financial years, set team capacity, and keep delivery on track with live countdowns.

Budgeting & Forecasting

Track estimates, forecasts, and actuals. Understand financial exposure across programmes and business units.

Reporting & Roadmaps

Generate reports and roadmaps across teams, programmes, and financial years for planning and executive reviews.

Teams & Permissions

Organise people into teams and business units with role-based access control and granular permission categories.
