Business Units

Represent your organisation's divisions for portfolio reporting and budget tracking.

Business Units are top-level organisational groupings — divisions, departments, or cost centres. Teams and budget allocations can be linked to a business unit, enabling portfolio views and reports to roll up by division.

Actions on this page

Button / ActionWhat it does
Add Business UnitCreate a new division. Enter a full name and a short abbreviation (max 10 characters) for badges and dashboards.
Click a rowOpens the business unit detail page — its name, code, and abbreviation on the left, and the list of Products belonging to it on the right.
Edit Update the name or abbreviation.
Deactivate / ActivateHides or shows the unit in reporting dropdowns. Existing team associations are preserved.
Delete Permanently removes the unit if no teams or allocations reference it.
Import / ExportBulk-create units from a spreadsheet, or download the current list.

How to use

  1. Go to Business Units and click Add Business Unit.
  2. Enter the full name (e.g. Finance & Operations) and a short abbreviation (e.g. FIN).
  3. Click Save. The unit now appears in team and reporting dropdowns.
  4. Click any row to open its detail page and see the Products associated with that business unit.
Tip: Keep abbreviations to 3–6 characters so they fit neatly in dashboard badges and report table columns without truncating.
